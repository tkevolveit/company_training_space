# Windows tips

## Making Chrome Open Automatically When Your Computer Boots Up
1. Press the Windows Logo Key + R on your keyboard to open the Run dialog box.
2. Type shell:startup into the box and press Enter to open the Windows Startup folder.
3. Create or copy a shortcut for Google Chrome from your desktop.
4. Paste the Chrome shortcut directly into the shell:startup folder that opened on your screen. Chrome will now launch automatically every time you turn on or log into your computer.
