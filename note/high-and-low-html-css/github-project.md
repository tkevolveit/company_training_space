## backlog
A backlog is an accumulation of unfinished tasks, unprocessed orders, or uncompleted work that needs to be dealt with.
Key Meanings

Unfinished Work: A buildup of tasks, emails, or requests that are overdue or waiting to be processed.

Project Management: A prioritized list of features, requirements, or tasks that a team needs to complete for a project.

Business and Orders: Unfilled customer orders or stock waiting to be shipped.Literal Origin: A large log placed at the back of a hearth fire to keep it burning slowly.
